# 04 — Home Screen Specification

**Version:** 2.0
**Status:** Approved
**Owner:** Norsk Trainer Team
**Last Updated:** 2026-06-25

---

# Purpose

The Home Screen is the starting point of Norsk Trainer.

Its role is not to expose every feature.

Its role is to guide today's learning.

---

# Core Question

Every time the application opens, the Home Screen should answer:

> **What should I do next?**

---

# Goals

The Home Screen should:

* welcome the user
* show learning progress
* recommend today's action
* provide quick access to learning
* motivate without overwhelming

---

# Layout

Top to bottom:

1. Greeting
2. Progress Hero Card
3. Today's Plan
4. Today's Statistics
5. Floating Tab Bar

The most important content should fit within the first screen whenever possible.

---

# Greeting

Displays:

* profile name
* welcome message
* current date (future)
* optional motivational message

Example:

Good evening, Anna.

Ready for today's lesson?

---

# Progress Hero

Purpose

Provide immediate understanding of long-term progress.

Displays:

* learned words
* current level
* overall completion
* streak (future)

---

# Today's Plan

The central card of the Home Screen.

Shows:

* due words
* today's recommendation
* estimated session length
* Start button

This is the primary action of the screen.

---

# Today's Statistics

Displays:

* today's progress
* accuracy
* completed exercises

Accessible at a glance.

---

# Analytics

Detailed analytics are intentionally hidden.

Access is provided through the Analytics button.

This keeps Home focused.

---

# Wallpaper

The wallpaper creates atmosphere but never distracts.

Glass components must remain readable.

---

# Personalization

The Home Screen reflects the user.

Supports:

* profile name
* language
* wallpaper
* theme
* glass style

Future:

* seasonal greetings
* achievements
* dynamic widgets

---

# Future Widgets

Potential additions:

* Weekly Goal
* Achievement of the Day
* New Words
* Calendar
* Daily Quote
* AI Recommendation

Widgets should remain optional.

---

# UX Rules

The Home Screen should:

* avoid scrolling
* present one primary action
* reduce cognitive load
* encourage learning
* feel calm

---

# Success Criteria

The user should understand the next action within three seconds.

No explanation should be required.

---

# Related Components

* HomeHeader
* ProgressHeroCard
* DailyPlanCard
* TodayStatsCard
* AnalyticsSheet
* FloatingTabBar

---

# Future Evolution

Future versions may include:

* dynamic widgets
* AI recommendations
* Apple Widgets
* Apple Watch sync
* family dashboard
* cloud learning summary

The Home Screen should evolve without losing its primary purpose.

---

**"The Home Screen should never ask users to think. It should simply invite them to learn."**
